## Demo-model generation record

Tool: OpenAI ImageGen via the Codex `imagegen` skill.

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: rights-safe demo input photo for
> an apparel virtual try-on web application. Create one original photorealistic
> studio photo of a fictional adult model suitable as a source image for an
> upper-body virtual try-on API. Use a plain seamless light warm-gray studio
> background with no objects. Show exactly one adult person facing directly
> forward, centered, looking at the camera, with a natural neutral expression,
> hair fully behind the shoulders, face completely unobstructed, both shoulders
> visible, arms relaxed slightly away from the torso, and standing upright.
> Frame from the top of the head through the hips so the entire upper body is
> visible. The person wears a simple close-fitting solid light-gray short-sleeve
> crew-neck shirt with no graphics. Use realistic commercial catalog
> photography with natural skin texture and no beauty retouching; vertical 3:4
> portrait; camera straight-on at chest height; subject occupying about 80% of
> frame; symmetrical composition; generous margin around head and arms; even
> soft studio lighting; minimal shadows; accurate neutral color. No jacket,
> accessories, jewelry, logos, brand marks, text, watermark, props, background
> objects, cropped anatomy, dramatic pose, or hand gestures.
