## Openfront

FitFront is a significant hackathon-focused enhancement of
[Openfront](https://github.com/openshiporg/openfront), an open-source e-commerce
platform by openship. Openfront is used under the MIT License reproduced in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE).

The FitFront contribution adds the YouCam virtual fitting-room workflow,
server-side API integration, privacy and quota controls, UI/branding, tests,
deployment documentation, and submission assets. Existing Openfront catalog,
cart, checkout, account, dashboard, GraphQL, and commerce-domain functionality
remains attributed to its upstream authors.
